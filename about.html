Family Offices & Business Organisations

Our aim is straightforward: fewer headaches, better hires, and teams that last.


Hiring internationally is time-consuming, high-risk and often frustrating and unnecessarily costly when partners don’t truly understand your market.


For over 22 years, SPS has worked with Private FO's and leading Businesses worldwide, helping them secure senior and specialist talent quickly, discreetly and for the long term.


We specialise in accessing hidden talent, experienced professionals who are not actively job hunting but are open to the right opportunity. This gives our clients access to candidates they simply won’t find through advertising alone.


We recruit across the Middle East, Asia, Africa, the USA and Bermuda, with deep, practical knowledge of local markets, relocation realities and retention challenges. Bermuda is a particular area of expertise. We have supported employers on the island for over two decades.
