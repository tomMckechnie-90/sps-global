Project Mgr, Estates & Facilities Mgmt

Location: Bermuda

Full Time

Salary: c$10k/month

Are you a qualified and experienced (7+ years) Project Manager, ideally a graduate with experience in/around Healthcare projects? Would you consider relocation to Bermuda? Working with our client based on the island, we would love to hear from you if so. It is likely that the successful applicant will have a BSc in a related field, as well as having Chartered status or similar. A relocation package is offered along with 3 month's initial accommodation if required and an almost tax-free salary of $124 per annum. Reporting to the Director of Estates & Facilities / Principal Engineer, the Project Manager – Estates and Facilities, is primarily responsible for the development, management and reporting of capital and operational projects within the Facilities Management and Estates Services Department.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Managing Facilities Management capital and operational projects, including planning, execution and commissioning
  • Managing internal resources, external consultants, contractors and other service providers to accomplish project objectives while ensuring operational efficiency
  • Preparing project documentation including business cases, specifications, schedules, contracts and construction documents
  • Managing all project expenditures according to financial instructions and other relevant financial controls
  • Managing assigned operating and capital budgets
  • Liaising with internal and external stakeholders, contractors and service providers to ensure effective service delivery
  • Effectively communicates with staff and customers on project developments and impacts to service delivery
  • Working with clinical professionals and other staff to ensure that the built environment can deliver high quality, effective and efficient patient care

Qualifications & Registration required:

  • Bachelor’s Degree (B. Sc.) or equivalent technical qualification in Engineering, Construction Management or Quantity Surveying
  • Diploma in Project Management or equivalent is essential
  • Minimum of seven (7) years of relevant post-graduate experience, with three (3) years at management level and Five (5) years of Contract and Construction Management experience
  • Three (3) years relevant post-Project Management qualification experience
  • Chartered or Registered Building Surveyor, Architect, Engineer or Project Management desirable
  • Liaising with internal and external stakeholders, contractors and service providers to ensure effective service delivery
  • Effectively communicates with staff and customers on project developments and impacts to service delivery
  • Experience within healthcare facilities is desirable
